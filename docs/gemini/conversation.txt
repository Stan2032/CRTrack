2. How to Handle Mid-Week Joiners Mathematically

Your instinct to measure equivalent activity is spot-on — and the math actually makes Prorated Medals + Deck Usage much cleaner and fairer than pure Win Rate %.

Why Win Rate % alone breaks down:

Clash Royale's War screen and API only expose Medals (fame) and Decks Used — not raw wins/losses. (You can calculate Medals per Deck, which is a clean skill proxy: 100 = all PvP losses, ~150–163 = ~50% wins, 200–225 = 100% wins.)

Clan Wars 2 rewards showing up far more than high win rate on low volume: a player who goes 1–0 and skips 3 games has a 100% win rate (200 medals), while someone who plays 4/4 decks and loses every single game earns double (400 medals).
