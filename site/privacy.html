Privacy

What War Audit keeps

From Clash Royale's public data (anyone can see it in game or on RoyaleAPI): players' names, tags, clan role, last online, donations, war medals, decks and war battles. Kept as the clan's war history, so the leaders can run the clan fairly.

From Discord, only if you use the bot: your Discord account linked to your player tag, and what you turn on (reminders, war reports, absences, applications, the leaders' digest).

Leaders' notes: decisions about each war (for example warnings and check-ins), and which leader changed what.

Leaders' phones: if a leader turns on alerts, the address their browser gives for notifications.

How it's used

Only to run the clan: war decisions, reminders, stats and the Discord server. It's stored with Cloudflare (Western Europe), and it's never sold or shared. The site makes no use of advertising or tracking cookies; your browser keeps your own settings (and a leader's code) on your device only.

Deleting it

In the Discord server: /privacy delete:True removes your Discord link and settings straight away (/unlink just disconnects). To have your stored war history removed too, ask a leader, who can use /leader forget. Public game data (the current members list and the war in progress) comes back while you're in the clan, as anyone can see it in game.

Questions: ask one of the clan's leaders, who run this tool.
