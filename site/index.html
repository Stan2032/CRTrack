Clan War Audit

How to use this
  1. Who to act on shows what to do after each war, in order. Tap the circle when a step is done. Kicks, demotions and promotions tick themselves once the game shows them.
  2. Copy gives a ready message for clan chat (each fits the 256-character limit).
  3. Tap a name for their history, reliability and battle record, or to change the suggestion.
  4. Last war / This war: on battle days, This war shows who still has decks to play, forecasts, and the race.
  5. Leaders: enter the leader code under Rules so your ticks and changes are shared, then turn on 🔔 alerts.
